✓ Before you submit: a quick checklist
  1. Scope fit beats rank. Read the aims and scope and skim the five most recent articles (shown in each journal's profile under "Recent articles").
  2. Cite the conversation. If your reference list has no papers from that journal, it is often a weak fit.
  3. Check speed and cost. First decision and acceptance times come from publisher pages; APCs only apply if you choose open access in hybrid journals.
  4. Check legitimacy using Think. Check. Submit. and confirm current indexing on Scopus/Web of Science before submitting.
  5. Plan a ladder. Pick one Reach, two Target and one Safer journal so a desk rejection does not cost you months.

About the data

Sources

    Add your own ranking list (e.g. AJG/CABS, FT50, UTD24, institutional list)

    Import any CSV or Excel file that has an ISSN column and a rating column. It is processed in your browser and saved only on this device; it then appears as a filter, a badge and a "similar journals" scale.

    Live lookups

    Journal profiles fetch live data from OpenAlex (free, open scholarly index): article counts, citations, APC list price, DOAJ status, research topics and the latest articles. If OpenAlex asks for a key, paste a free one here.

    Notes and caveats

    • Rankings and metrics change every year. Always confirm on the official source before making decisions.
    • Publishing timelines (first decision, review, acceptance, publication) are medians/averages published by Elsevier and Springer Nature on their journal pages, collected on 27 Oct 2026. Other publishers do not publish them consistently; use the profile links to check.
    • Journals are matched across lists by ISSN / eISSN. A small number of title changes or ISSN errors in source lists may cause mismatches.
